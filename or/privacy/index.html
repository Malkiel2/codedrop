ଗୋପନୀୟତା

ପ୍ରଭାବୀ ତାରିଖ: 2026-11-06

In short: the text of your messages never leaves your iPhone. Only the verification code is sent, encrypted, directly to the computers you paired, over your own network. There is no CodeDrop server, no account and no analytics.

This policy explains what the CodeDrop apps for iPhone, Mac and Windows, and this website, do with your information. CodeDrop is provided by Malkiel Shaul (“we”).

1. Your messages and codes

  • When the CodeDrop automation you switched on in Shortcuts runs, it gives CodeDrop the text of the new message. CodeDrop looks for a verification code on your iPhone. The message text is not stored, not logged and never sent anywhere.
  • If a code is found, only that code is sent, and only to the computers you paired. It is encrypted end to end (X25519 key agreement and ChaCha20-Poly1305 encryption, with separate keys for each paired computer) and travels directly over your local network. It does not pass through any CodeDrop server, because there isn’t one.
  • On your computer, CodeDrop places the code on the clipboard so you can paste it. It stays there until you copy something else, like anything you copy yourself. Other apps on your computer that can read the clipboard can see it, as with any copied text. On Windows, CodeDrop asks Windows to keep the code out of clipboard history and cloud clipboard sync.

2. Pairing

To pair, you scan a QR code shown by the computer app. The camera is used on your iPhone only to read that QR code; no images are stored or sent. During pairing your iPhone and computer exchange device names (for example the name of your iPhone) and encryption keys. These are stored only on your devices (on Apple devices, in the Keychain) so they can recognise each other. You can remove a paired device at any time on either side.

CodeDrop asks for Local Network access on your iPhone so it can find and reach your computer on your Wi‑Fi. On Windows, it asks you to allow it through the firewall for the same reason.

3. Data stored on your devices

  • Activity log. The iPhone app keeps a short history of what CodeDrop did (for example “sent to your Mac” or “nothing found”). It never contains codes or message text. It holds at most 150 events, nothing older than 30 days, and you can clear it at any time. It is shared only if you choose to export diagnostics and send them yourself.
  • Free-code counter. The number of free automatic codes you have used is kept in your iPhone’s Keychain.
  • Settings and pairings stay on your devices.

4. What we don’t do

  • No CodeDrop account and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and no selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No CodeDrop server receives your messages, codes or device information.

5. Purchases

CodeDrop Pro is sold through the Apple App Store. Apple processes your payment; we never see your payment details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to your App Store purchase.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID created by the app (not your name or email), your purchase and transaction data, and basic device and app information (such as the app version, operating system and store country), so it can confirm what you bought. See RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

When the Windows app is available in the Microsoft Store, downloads and any purchases there are handled by Microsoft under Microsoft’s privacy policy.

6. Notifications

CodeDrop’s notifications (for example “Code not copied to your computer” or “1 free code left”) are created on your device. They never contain your code or message text and are not sent through a push server.

7. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party trackers. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep them secure.

8. Children

CodeDrop is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your choices and rights

Because your data stays on your devices, you control it: clear the activity log, remove paired devices, or delete the apps to remove their data (the free-code counter stays in the Keychain until the device is erased). For data held by Apple, Microsoft or RevenueCat, see their policies. If you have a privacy question or request, contact us.

10. Features of your operating system

CodeDrop relies on features of iOS, macOS, Windows and the Shortcuts app that Apple and Microsoft provide and may change or remove at any time. If a platform change stops part of CodeDrop from working, we will not replace it with a method that collects more of your data without telling you first and updating this policy. See also our Terms of Use.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update it here and change the effective date. If a change means more data leaves your devices, we’ll tell you in the app first.
