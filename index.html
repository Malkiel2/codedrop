    If CodeDrop isn’t sure it found the right code, it sends nothing. Promo codes aren’t sent.

Honest limits

What CodeDrop needs to work today:

  • Same Wi‑Fi. Your iPhone and computer must be on the same network, and the network must let devices talk to each other. Many office, hotel and guest networks don’t.
  • CodeDrop running on the computer. It can start with your computer and lives in the menu bar or system tray.
  • Unlock once after a restart. After your iPhone restarts, unlock it once. After that it works while locked.
  • iOS 27 or later on your iPhone.
  • Text messages only. CodeDrop works with codes that arrive in the Messages app.

Questions

Do I have to copy the code?

No. CodeDrop copies it to your computer automatically. You just paste with ⌘V or Ctrl+V.

Does my message go to a server?

No. The message is read on your iPhone and never leaves it. Only the code is sent, encrypted, straight to your own computers over your Wi‑Fi. There is no CodeDrop server and no account.

Why do I have to switch the automation on myself?

Apple requires it. CodeDrop gives you a ready-made automation, but only you can turn it on. CodeDrop can’t see whether it’s on.

Does it work when my iPhone is locked?

Yes. Unlock it once after a restart; after that, codes copy themselves while it’s locked.

Does it work over Bluetooth or mobile data?

Not today. Your iPhone and computer need to be on the same Wi‑Fi network.

Why doesn’t it work at my office or hotel?

Many office, hotel and guest networks block devices from talking to each other. Use your home or a personal network.

Can I use more than one computer?

Yes. Pair as many Macs and PCs as you like. Each code is copied to all of them.

How long does the code stay on my clipboard?

Until you copy something else, like any other text you copy. CodeDrop never clears your clipboard on a timer.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. The Mac and Windows apps are built so Android phones can be added later.

I bought Pro on another device or reinstalled. How do I restore it?

In the iPhone app, open Purchases & restore and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Windows asks about the firewall. What do I choose?

Choose Allow. Your iPhone needs to reach CodeDrop on your PC over your Wi‑Fi.
