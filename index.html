For iPhone, with Mac and Windows

Your verification codes copy themselves.

From phone to paste. Instantly.

When a code arrives on your iPhone, CodeDrop copies it to your computer automatically. You don’t open the message. You don’t copy. You don’t type. Just paste.

Get CodeDropSee how it works

Set up once. Then just paste.

A short, one-time setup. From then on, codes copy themselves.

  1. 1

    Pair your computer

    Install CodeDrop on your Mac or Windows PC. Scan the QR code it shows with your iPhone and check the 6-digit number matches.

  2. 2

    Switch the automation on

    Add the ready-made CodeDrop automation in Shortcuts, tap Edit, turn Automation on and leave Notify off. Apple requires you to do this one step yourself.

  3. 3

    Just paste

    When a code arrives, it’s already on your computer’s clipboard. Press ⌘V on a Mac or Ctrl+V on Windows.

Get CodeDrop

Install CodeDrop on your iPhone, then on each computer you want codes on.
