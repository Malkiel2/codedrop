Support

Everything you need to set up CodeDrop and keep it working.

Set up CodeDrop

  1. 1

    Install CodeDrop on your computer

    Get CodeDrop for Mac or Windows from the download page and open it. It shows a QR code.

  2. 2

    Pair your iPhone

    In the CodeDrop iPhone app, scan the QR code. If your iPhone asks for Local Network access, tap Allow. Check that the 6-digit number matches on both screens.

  3. 3

    Add the automation

    Tap Add Automation, then Add Shortcut in Shortcuts.

  4. 4

    Switch it on

    Open the CodeDrop automation and tap Edit. Turn Automation on and leave Notify off. Then come back to CodeDrop.

  5. 5

    Try it

    Tap Send a test code. Then wait for your next real code; don’t open the message. Paste with ⌘V or Ctrl+V.

Troubleshooting

Nothing was copied

Check that your iPhone and computer are on the same Wi‑Fi, CodeDrop is running on the computer, and the automation is on. Then tap Check Connections in the iPhone app.

“Your computer didn’t accept this iPhone”

Pair again: scan the QR code on your computer once more.

Local Network is off

On your iPhone, open Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network and turn on CodeDrop.

Office, hotel or guest Wi‑Fi

These networks often block devices from talking to each other. Use your home or a personal network.

After my iPhone restarts

Unlock it once. After that, codes copy themselves while it’s locked.

A message had no code, or the wrong one

CodeDrop only sends a code when it’s confident. If it isn’t sure, nothing is sent and you can copy the code by hand.

Windows asks about the firewall

Choose Allow.

Restore purchases

In the iPhone app, open Purchases & restore and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought CodeDrop Pro with. To cancel or change a subscription, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
